# Aurora Travel Policy

## Booking flights
Book economy for flights under six hours. Longer flights may
be booked in premium economy with manager approval.

## Meals and per diem
Domestic trips have a meal allowance of $50 per day.
International trips have a meal allowance of $75 per day.
Alcohol is not covered by either allowance.

## Ground transport
Use rideshare or public transit where available. Rental cars
need advance approval.
